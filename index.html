HOW IT WORKS

1. NOR memory: Each pair of cross-coupled NOR gates stores one bit. SET=1 stores Q=1; RESET=1 stores Q=0. SET=RESET=1 is forbidden.

2. XNOR comparison: Four separate XNOR gates compare stored Q with the DIP switches.

3. AND decision: Three two-input AND gates combine all four equality outputs.

4. Driver: AND HIGH drives the NPN base through a resistor. The transistor energizes the 5V relay coil. A flyback diode is connected across the coil. Relay NO contacts switch the separate 12V solenoid supply.
